## Best-Fit Track

Primary track: **Payments & Wallets**

Official positioning on the site: build payment rails or wallet infrastructure for the decentralized web and apps on Sui.

SuiTrustPay fits because it is a payment and commerce rail with:

- hosted checkout
- Sui wallet execution
- on-chain escrow
- settlement abstraction
- merchant SDK/API

## Secondary Narrative

Specialized track / sponsor narrative: **Walrus**

Official positioning on the site: build applications that handle large, off-chain, or verifiable data.

SuiTrustPay fits this narrative because dispute evidence is stored off-chain, while Walrus blob IDs and content hashes are committed to Sui objects.
